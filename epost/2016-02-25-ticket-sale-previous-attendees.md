Dear previous Web Rebels attendee!

This is the 5th year of Web Rebels and to celebrate we will have three conferences!  We got CSSconf Nordic on June 1st, then Web Rebels between June 2nd and 3rd, ending with NodeConf Oslo on June 4th. 

All three events will be at Vulkan in Grünerløkka, Oslo, but we are moving to a bigger venue 50 meters down the road from previous years; Dansens Hus. This will give us a chance to add a bit more attendees and we hope this will make it easier to get tickets for those which have not previous years. But fear not; we will still be a small and intimate conference with all the love and fun we’ve had previous years. 

Talking about attending, our first batch of tickets are going on sale this Friday (February 26th) at 13:00 CET: 
https://www.webrebels.org/tickets

Interested in buying tickets for more than one event? Excellent, because all three events are releasing tickets at the same place and at the same time. There will also be combo-tickets available.

At Web Rebels we are always aiming for a good mix of talks with practical information, deep insight and a dash of craziness. Our first speakers are already announced and we have more in the pipeline: 
https://www.webrebels.org/speakers
