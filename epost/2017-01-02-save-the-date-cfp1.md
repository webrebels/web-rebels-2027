Web Rebels 2017 — save the date

We’re happy to announce this year’s dates: the conference is going to happen on 1 – 2 June. We have chosen to stick to the same location, and keep the temperature at an acceptable level. Pinky promise.

Ticket sales will open in spring, so keep your eyes peeled to your inbox and our Twitter stream for announcements.
Call for Papers — submit your abstracts before midnight (CET) on Jan 30th

Traditionally, Web Rebels has been invite-only for speakers. This time, for the first time, we open up for submissions from the community. We’re pretty excited about this, and hope to see a lot of you to send in abstracts. The final line-up will be a mix of speakers invited by us and speakers chosen from the CfP.

We particularly encourage people new to public speaking and members of underrepresented groups in tech to send in their suggestions.

Get all the details on our website.

Please spread the word, encourage others to submit, and shoot us an email at kontor@webrebels.org about anyone you really think we should invite. Or hit us up on Twitter.
Sponsoring — help make Web Rebels happen again

Web Rebels is a not-for-profit organization, and as such we rely on sponsorships to make it all happen. At this point, we still have some sponsoring opportunities available for 2017. You can check out our list of available options on our website.

Get in touch at sponsors@webrebels.org if you’re interested.
