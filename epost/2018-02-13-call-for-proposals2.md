2018 Call for Proposals

Our call for proposals is still open until 25th February. If you have an idea for a presentation, you should absolutely submit. The final line-up will be a mix of invited speakers and those selected from the CfP.

We will cover travel and accommodation for all accepted speakers, as well as their ticket to the conference, and we will invite them to our traditional speakers' dinner.

We specifically encourage first-time speakers to apply, we'll do our best to make you shine on stage.

Head over to Papercall.io to submit your proposal.
