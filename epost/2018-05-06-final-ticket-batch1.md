Ticket sale

The first two batches are sold out, and it went faster than expected. So we decided to do a third ticket drop again this year.

The final batch will go out this Monday, May 7th, at 17:00 Oslo time (CEST). Set your alarms, and head over to webrebels.org/tickets when it’s time.

Speaker line-up 

This year's program is looking dope. We could add nine more excellent speakers to our line-up over the last couple of days. They will cover a broad range of topics from performance to 3D rendering to crypto to VR to hacking sex toys. And it's all about Javascript and web technologies.

Head over to webrebels.org/speakers for details and watch @web_rebels for more speaker announcements.

Diversity scholarships

Thanks to those wonderful people from the community who bought diversity support tickets, and a generous donation from Mozilla, we are able to provide members from underrepresented groups in tech with free access to the conference again.

If you believe you qualify, apply via this form. Everyone else, please spread the word, and nudge a person you know to apply.
